# Incident Response Runbook

Incidents are classified into four severity levels. Sev1 means a full outage or a security breach affecting customer data. Sev2 means a major feature is broken for a large subset of users. Sev3 means a minor feature is degraded. Sev4 means a cosmetic or low-impact issue with a known workaround.

Sev1 incidents page the on-call engineer and the engineering manager within 15 minutes of detection, and require a incident commander to be assigned before any remediation work begins. Sev2 incidents page only the on-call engineer, with a 30 minute response target.

Every Sev1 and Sev2 incident requires a written postmortem within five business days of resolution. The postmortem must include a timeline, root cause, and at least one action item with an owner and a due date.

If an incident involves potential exposure of customer personal data, the security team and legal must be looped in immediately regardless of severity level, and the data handling policy's breach notification procedure takes over.
