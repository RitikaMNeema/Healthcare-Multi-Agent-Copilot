# Appeals Policy

A first-level appeal must be filed within 180 days of the denial notice date. If the first-level appeal is upheld, a second-level appeal must be filed within 60 days of the first-level decision. If the second-level appeal is also upheld, the claim may proceed to an independent external review within 120 days, where available under the payer's plan type.

Every appeal must include: a copy of the original claim, the denial notice, and documentation specific to the denial category - a corrected field for administrative denials, a letter of medical necessity for medical-necessity denials, or a retroactive authorization number for authorization denials.

PR-1 (patient deductible) denials are not eligible for provider appeal, since the denial reflects patient financial responsibility rather than a payer determination.

Appeals for timely-filing denials (CO-29) are rarely successful and should only be filed when the provider has documented proof of on-time submission, such as a clearinghouse transmission report with a timestamp before the filing deadline.

Appeal outcomes are tracked as overturned (the original denial is reversed and the claim is paid) or upheld (the denial stands). A claim with three or more denials for the same code within a 12-month period should be escalated to the payer relations team regardless of appeal outcome.
