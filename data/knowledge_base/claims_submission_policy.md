# Claims Submission Policy

A clean claim must include the patient's demographic and insurance information, the rendering provider's NPI, the correct procedure and diagnosis codes, the date of service, and the billed amount. Claims missing any of these fields will be rejected before adjudication and do not count against timely filing deadlines until resubmitted.

Medicare requires claims to be filed within 365 days of the date of service. Commercial payers - including Aetna, BlueCross BlueShield, and UnitedHealthcare - require timely filing within 90 days of the date of service. A claim submitted after the applicable window is denied with code CO-29 and is not eligible for a timely-filing exception except in cases of documented payer system outages.

Corrected claims (resubmissions fixing an administrative error) must reference the original claim number and be submitted within 60 days of the original denial notice, or they are treated as new claims subject to the standard timely filing window.

Electronic claims submitted through a clearinghouse receive an acknowledgment within 24 hours; paper claims are not acknowledged and should be tracked manually by the submitting provider's billing team.
